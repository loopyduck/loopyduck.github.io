About the game

Rivermarch is a full real-time strategy game made for a touchscreen. Gather grain, wood, gold and stone, build a town, train an army and march it across the river. Win by burning the enemy's Hall, or by holding both river shrines until the clock runs out.

Built for fingers, not a mouse

Tap a unit to select it, hold and drag to gather a group, tap an enemy to attack. Your last two groups wait on their own buttons, one tap away. Big buttons find your idle workers, your army and your buildings in one tap. A guided lesson teaches every control in a few minutes.

Four peoples, four ways to win

  • Highmark: stone walls, sturdy towers and Bastion Guards who shrug off arrows.
  • Sunreach: rich traders with herds of war elephants and Coin Blades who earn gold from every kill.
  • Greenwood: deadly archers and Thornstalkers who vanish among the trees.
  • Windrider: fast riders and Dust Lancers whose charge hits three times as hard.

200 troops a side

Battles on a scale few phone games attempt. Every player, with Plus or without, can field up to 200 troops, online and against the computer. Choose 100 or 200 before each match; online, you meet players who chose the same.

An army worth commanding

Swordsmen, archers, cavalry, Pikeriders, Bowriders, war elephants with archers on their backs, catapults, trebuchets, scorpions and rams. Every unit has its strengths and its counters, and armor that really matters.

Play online

Find an opponent near your rating, or challenge a friend through Game Center. Climb the ladder, top the weekly board and earn Crowns for every match.

Matches that fit your day

Most games last 10 to 20 minutes. Play the computer at four levels, or survive waves of Raiders and set a best time.

Looks only, never power

Spend the Crowns you win on banner crests, decorations for your Hall and picture frames, with new pieces every season. Nothing in the Shop changes how anything fights.

Rivermarch Plus

One purchase, no subscription, no ads. Plus adds unlimited online matches, the Extreme computer and Huge maps. Every player, with Plus or without, fields armies of up to 200 troops, so Plus never buys an advantage over another player.
